# Style and review defaults

Use these as starting points, not a required story, layout, palette, or UI treatment.

- Use semantic color roles. Start with a warm paper or cool workspace treatment when useful.
- Show one focal action per beat. Hold the result long enough to read.
- Use real screenshots when they clarify the story. Label reconstructed behavior honestly.
- Prefer restrained depth and clear step cues over dense decoration.
- At article mobile width, crop closer or simplify. Do not shrink unreadable desktop UI.
- Review the beginning, each main beat, transitions, and final state as stills.
- Watch the encoded clip at normal speed. Check playback, duration, dimensions, and decode errors.
- Treat smoothness and visual approval as human review. Frame rate alone is not proof.
